Privacy Policy

NIL SparkLab is a free educational electrical & electronics virtual lab. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app stores, what stays only on your device, and how the optional AI/search features work.

No account needed No ads No data sold No server-side storage of your activity

1. Summary

You can use the entire Circuit Builder, component library, industrial lab, fault finder, and quizzes without creating an account and without any of your activity being sent anywhere. Data leaves your device only when you actively use an optional Smart Assistant/search feature, and even then it is only used to fetch an answer — it is not stored on our servers.

2. What is stored on your device only

NIL SparkLab uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) to save, on your own device:

  • Your selected language (English/Hindi).
  • Your recent Circuit Builder layout, so you don't lose work on refresh.
  • Your Smart Assistant chat history, so you can scroll back during a session.

This information never leaves your browser, is not visible to us, and is not synced to any account. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for NIL SparkLab.

3. What happens when you use the Smart Assistant / search tools

The Web Search, Research Search, Literature Search, Book Search, and AI Assistant features are optional. When you use one of these:

  1. Your query text is sent from your browser to NIL SparkLab's own gateway/Worker.
  2. The gateway forwards only what's needed to the relevant third-party provider to fetch a result: Google Gemini (AI answers), Wikimedia/Wikipedia (web search), Crossref and Europe PMC (research/literature metadata), or Open Library (book search).
  3. The result is returned to your browser and shown to you.

NIL SparkLab's gateway does not log, store, or retain your query text or the responses — it acts only as a pass-through. However, each third-party provider processes the request under its own privacy policy, since the request reaches their servers:

  • Google (Gemini) Privacy Policy
  • Wikimedia Privacy Policy
  • Crossref Privacy Policy
  • Europe PMC Privacy Notice
  • Open Library Privacy Policy

Please avoid typing personal or sensitive information into the Smart Assistant.

4. Analytics

NIL SparkLab may record basic, anonymous, on-device usage counters (for example, which tools are opened) purely to help improve the app. This data is not tied to your identity, is not shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising.

5. Cookies and advertising

NIL SparkLab does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and does not sell or share personal data with advertisers.

6. Children's privacy

NIL SparkLab is an educational tool suitable for students. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, beyond the on-device storage described above.

7. Data security

Because your activity is stored locally and the search/AI gateway does not retain query data, there is no central database of user activity to be breached. Standard transport security (HTTPS) protects data in transit to the gateway and third-party providers.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as features change. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of NIL SparkLab after an update means you accept the revised policy.
